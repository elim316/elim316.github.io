INTRODUCTION

Bridging cloud infrastructure, backend systems, and applied AI.

I am a Cloud and AI Engineer at Google in Singapore, where I architect zero-trust cloud landing zones for generative AI agents, build native observability plugins to visualise multi-agent execution trajectories, and run hands-on buildathons that help engineering teams take AI workloads into production.

Before joining Google, I worked as a Trust Software Engineer on Grab's Trust, Identity and Safety team building Golang fraud-detection services on petabyte-scale data infrastructure, and as an AI Research Intern at A*STAR developing uncertainty-aware and explainable deep learning models for battery State-of-Health estimation. I graduated with a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science (Second Upper Class) from the University of Glasgow and the Singapore Institute of Technology.

I enjoy working across the full stack and collaborating closely with both technical and non-technical stakeholders to ship dependable software. Outside of work, you will usually find me playing basketball or pickleball, or hunting for vintage pieces while thrifting.
